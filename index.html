About Us

ArtBeat is an independent exhibition series dedicated to contemporary and emerging voices.

We stage exhibitions that pair established practices with new work, turning raw spaces into places for encounter. Each edition is built around a single idea and unfolds over a season of openings, talks, and performances.

Our Approach

We believe art belongs outside the white cube as much as within it. ArtBeat commissions, curates, and collaborates with artists to present work in its most direct form — unmediated, immediate, and alive.
